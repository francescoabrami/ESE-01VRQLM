\subsection{Introduction}


The main purpose of this exercise is to become familiar with the equipment and measurement procedures used in the fields of electronics and integrated systems.

\subsection*{Goal}

The objective of this laboratory is to become familiar with electronic measurement techniques through practical use of a Digital Storage Oscilloscope (DSO).
In the first part we will briefly go over some calibration practices to then move on to the analog and digital sections.

\subsection*{Instrument}

Here is the list of the different instruments and equipment used during this laboratory.
